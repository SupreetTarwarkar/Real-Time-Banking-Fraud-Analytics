# Business Insights

Findings from the synthetic banking transaction data and the Microsoft Fabric / Power BI dashboards. The July–September baseline contains **240,000 transactions**; the expanded July–November simulation contains **409,000**. These are controlled test datasets, not observed banking activity.

## Key findings

1. **Alert counts and alert rates reveal different patterns.** September had the most flagged transactions (**3,807 alerts** across **94,000 transactions**), while August had the highest alert rate (**4.12%**). Comparing counts alone would overstate risk in high-volume months.

2. **Payment frequency is not the same as financial exposure.** In the July–September baseline, **UPI contributed 44.19% of transactions but only 20.46% of transaction value**. Credit Card and Net Banking each accounted for nearly a quarter of value despite much smaller transaction shares. Channel monitoring should consider both frequency and amount.

3. **Multiple rule matches identify cases for closer review.** The expanded simulation produced **796 multi-scenario alerts** among **2,465 Critical alerts**. Preserving the contributing rules and reasons makes those cases easier to investigate. A Critical label indicates rule severity, not confirmed fraud.

4. **Scenario and channel exposure are concentrated in different segments.** The expanded dashboard displays **₹28.79 Cr** for Spending Burst scenario exposure and **₹30.38 Cr** for Credit Card channel exposure. These category-level amounts should **not be added together** or treated as reconciled components of the **₹45.28 Cr** headline exposure: the report's category totals still require validation.

5. **Geographic alert volume and exposure point to different locations.** Maharashtra recorded the most state-level alerts (**1,286**), New Delhi the most city-level alerts (**476**), and Bengaluru the highest displayed city exposure (**₹1.42 Cr**). A location with more alerts is not necessarily the location with the largest estimated amount at risk.

6. **Risk patterns vary by payment channel in the simulation.** The scenario heatmap highlights High Velocity and Impossible Travel under UPI, Spending Burst under Credit Card, and Unusual High Value under Net Banking. These results reflect the synthetic generator and configured KQL rules; they are not population-level fraud estimates.

7. **Operational monitoring complements historical reporting.** One captured rolling-hour dashboard showed **282 transactions**, **14 alerts**, **1 Critical alert**, and a **4.96% alert rate**. This is a single monitoring snapshot, not an additional month of historical data.

## Interpretation and limitations

The expanded dataset's overall alert rate is **3.97%** (**16,244 alerts** across **409,000 transactions**), compared with **4.03%** in the historical baseline. These periods overlap, so this is not an independent before-and-after comparison.

All alerts are generated by **rule-based KQL** against simulated transactions. Estimated potential exposure is **not verified financial loss**. Some exposure totals and detection results still require reconciliation.
